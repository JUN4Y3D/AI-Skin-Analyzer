HOW IT WORKS

One photograph, four distinct experimental signals.

  1. Your browser sends the selected image to this Mac’s localhost server.No cloud inference or analytics service is used.
  2. The app validates the image and requires one detected face.OpenCV creates one in-memory facial crop; ambiguous or missing faces produce a recovery message.
  3. Every fitted component analyzes that same crop once.ResNet50 returns provisional skin-type scores, an age-like label estimate, and two positive-reference similarities.

None of these outputs is a diagnosis, measured physiology, or independently validated clinical result.
