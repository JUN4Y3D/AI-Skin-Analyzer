# Privacy and limitations

For the AI Skin Analyzer 2.0 local application, see [LOCAL_APP_PRIVACY.md](LOCAL_APP_PRIVACY.md) and [MODEL_LIMITATIONS.md](MODEL_LIMITATIONS.md).

Treat face images, filenames, annotations, private manifests, reports and model outputs as sensitive. Keep them local and access-controlled; do not upload them to GitHub, CI, services or model providers. Public availability does not establish training or redistribution rights. Consent, minor-related terms, retention and deletion responsibilities require source-specific review.

The release checker is a local screening aid, not a guarantee: it requires the private filename inventory and fails closed for unknown types or inaccessible input. Human review remains mandatory for semantic identifiers, derived biometric data, rights, and embedded content. No face recognition or identity inference is used. No clinical validity or independent test performance has been established.
